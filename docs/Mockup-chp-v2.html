Getting around Concreat Connect

A quick guide to each section of the portal and what you can do there. The section you're currently on is shown bold and underlined at the top of the page.

Dashboard

View a quick overview of your account balance, account status, and Sales Orders by status. Select any tile to view the related orders.

Delivery Sites

View delivery sites approved for your account. Request a new site or changes to an existing site, and track the status of your request.

Orders

Place Single, Blanket, or Multiple Orders; view, reorder, filter, and download order details from your order history in CSV or Excel.

Customer Ledger

View posted invoices, credit memos, and payments on your account. Filter the information you need and download it in CSV or Excel format.

Document Center

Download invoices, credit memos, receipts, and generated Statements of Account anytime.

Requests

Submit requests for assistance or service, and track their status through resolution.

Complaints & Feedback

Submit a complaint, respond to a service survey, or commend an employee for good service.

Account and User Management

View your registered company details and manage who can access the portal and what they can see.
