Getting around Concreat Connect

A quick guide to what each section of the portal is for.

Dashboard

A quick greeting, your account balance and credit standing, and a live snapshot of your Sales Orders by status — click any tile to jump straight to those orders.

Delivery Sites

The sites approved for delivery on your account. Request a new site, or a change to one already here, and track that request until it's approved.

Orders

Place a Single or Blanket Order, submit multiple orders at once, and track every order from submission through delivery and invoicing.

Customer Ledger

Your account's posted invoices, credit memos, and payments — the same figures reflected on the Dashboard.

Document Center

Download invoices, credit memos, receipts, and a generated Statement of Account, any time.

Requests

Ask your account team about a sales order, a trip, a delivery, or a document copy, and track it through to resolution.

Complaints & Feedback

File a complaint about an order, delivery, or service, and track its status until it's resolved.

Account and User Management

Your registered company details, and who on your team can sign in to the portal and what they can see.
